Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026  |  Developer: Sanskar Joshi  |  Package ID: com.sanskarjoshi.echogrid

This Privacy Policy explains how Echo Grid ("the App", "the Game") handles information when you play on Android or on the web. By installing or using Echo Grid, you agree to the practices described on this page.

1. Information We Collect Directly

We do not require user accounts, logins, or registration, and we do not directly collect, store, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Your gameplay progress—such as unlocked levels, star ratings, and audio settings—is stored strictly locally on your device using browser/WebView local storage. This data is never uploaded to our own servers.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The Android version of Echo Grid displays optional Rewarded video ads and Interstitial ads through Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to keep the game free to play. To deliver, personalize, and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app-set identifiers.
  • Network & Approximate Location: IP address (used to estimate general region/country).
  • App Interaction & Ad Performance: Ad views, clicks, and rewarded ad completion events.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Data: Performance metrics and SDK crash logs.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data when you use partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Your Privacy Choices & Opt-Out Controls

  • Manage or Delete Advertising ID: On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads at any time by opening Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Delete Local Game Data: Because game progress is saved only on your device, you can permanently erase all Echo Grid local data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

4. Children's Privacy

Echo Grid is designed for general audiences aged 12 and older and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will promptly address the request.

5. Security

We do not operate external player databases. Any ad-related communications handled by Google AdMob use standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
